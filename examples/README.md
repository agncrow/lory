# Examples

Each example is a Crow program that builds for the web
(`crow build --target web main.co` in its folder). On Lory's site, each name
below opens the example running in your browser. In a headset's browser the
examples that have a headset part offer to enter VR. In the repository, each
name opens the example's source.

| Example | What it shows |
|---|---|
| [shapes](shapes/) | A rounded panel, a ring that turns with the frame's time, lines, and text at three sizes. |
| [controls](controls/) | A form beside a table, every control answered from the frame's input, and a drawn rating. In a headset, a field is typed in on a drawn keyboard. |
| [colors](colors/) | Every scale of Lory's colours, light and dark, each with the contrast of its text steps. Press a scale's name to make it the theme's accent or gray, and a few controls beside the scales take the theme. |
| [motion](motion/) | Easings drawn as curves, a ball on a spring, a staggered grid and a timeline with its playhead: play, pause, reverse and seek it. Press a curve to see it large. |
| [icons](icons/) | Every icon of Lory's set, at 24 and 15 pixels with its name, in the light look and the dark. Scroll with the wheel, the keys or a drag. |
| [datatable](datatable/) | Three hundred made-up people in a table: search as you type, sort by a column's button (again to turn it round), page ten, twenty-five or fifty at a time, group by team or city with counts, mean ages and total scores, and select people. In a headset, the ray presses and the thumbstick scrolls. |
| [datagrid](datagrid/) | A grid to edit, with rules and a Save; cells chosen in ranges and merged; TOAST UI Grid's keys, copy and paste; a live feed whose changed numbers flash; and a spreadsheet with formulas that recalculate. In a headset, the ray chooses a cell and the thumbstick scrolls. |
| [forms](forms/) | A form checked as Semantic UI checks it (sent empty, six errors and the focus on the first), a combobox, a masked phone number, a one-time code, a password shown or hidden, a stepper, a range slider, stars, switches, toggles, cards, a colour picker, a Markdown editor with its preview and a small image editor. In a headset, stars are pressed by the ray and the code typed on a drawn keyboard. |
| [map](map/) | A map of OpenStreetMap's tiles: drag it, zoom with the wheel or the buttons, fly to a city, open a marker's popup, and see points gathered into clusters that open as you zoom in. In a headset, the trigger presses and the thumbstick zooms. |
| [display](display/) | An app bar, breadcrumbs, a stepper, pages of cards, a toolbar, chips, avatars and badges, a timeline, statistics, skeletons, spinners and loaders, keys and shortcuts, highlighted words, alerts and a gallery whose pictures open in a lightbox. In a headset, the ray presses. |
| [menus](menus/) | A menubar with submenus, a context menu, a command palette, a dialog, a popover, a hover card, tooltips, toasts and a sheet; an accordion and a tree with checkboxes. In a headset, the ray opens and chooses. |
| [pickers](pickers/) | A day in a field with its calendar, an icon, a cross and a Today button; a stay chosen over two months; a calendar inline with the weeks' numbers, marked days and a holiday; a month and a year; a list of times; and a week of meetings that overlap. In a headset, a day is pressed by the ray. |
| [carousel](carousel/) | react-slick's carousels in all their kinds (arrows, dots, autoplay, loops, rows, centred, fading, lazy, upright, two moving each other), Vuetify's window and slide group, and Motion's endless ticker. Swipe with the mouse, or in a headset with the ray. |
| [charts](charts/) | A gallery of every kind of chart Chart.js draws, drawn by Kite: hide a dataset from its legend, see a mark's tooltip, and pick new data to see the marks move. |
| [scrolling](scrolling/) | Full-page sections with dots, slides and arrows; a scroll area with its own bar; a list of 50,000 rows; a feed pulled down to refresh; a sticky heading, reveals, parallax and a scroll spy. In a headset, the thumbstick scrolls. |
| [drag](drag/) | Lists reordered and items carried between them (by a grip, from a palette, swapped, several at once, in a grid), a control thrown about, cards swiped away, mail rows swiped to their actions, two pictures compared, a picture pinched, and a honeycomb. |
| [models](models/) | Six model files (OBJ with MTL, PLY, STL, XYZ, VTK, OFF) read as three.js reads them and drawn: drag to turn one, and see the file Lory writes of it as OBJ, STL, PLY or glTF. |
| [sound](sound/) | Sound rendered by Lory's sound package as Tone.js renders it: choose a synth and an effect, turn the volume, play the keyboard, and run a step sequencer, with a meter and a spectrum. Its sound needs Crow's newest page loader. |
| [vue](vue/) | Vue's 20 official examples built in Lory, from a menu in Vue's groups: Hello World, bindings, a Markdown editor, fetched commits, a sortable grid, a tree, an SVG graph, a modal, list transitions, and the seven GUIs (counter, temperature, flights, timer, CRUD, circles, cells). |
| [solid](solid/) | Solid's own examples built in Lory, from a menu: the counter, the todos, a clock, a sketch, routing, context, fetching with suspense and more. |
| [lit](lit/) | Lit's playground examples built in Lory, by its groups: templates, directives, context and signals, its React wrappers, and its motion examples (a circle, a list, a grid, a carousel, a hero). |
| [chart](chart/) | A year of one-minute candles (half a million bars) with volume and a moving average. Drag to pan and use the wheel to zoom, with a crosshair. |
| [kite](kite/) | Kite's chart: candles, a moving average, volume and the MACD in panes. Drag to scroll, use the wheel to zoom, and drag the price axis to stretch it. |
| [scanner](scanner/) | Kite's scanner card: thirty-second bars with VWAPs, a band, volume and a percentage ladder. |
| [geometry](geometry/) | Every core shape of three.js, laid out as three lays it out: choose a shape, change its numbers with sliders, and see it solid, by its edges or as a wireframe. Drag to turn it and use the wheel to come nearer. |
| [threejs](threejs/) | three.js's manual and examples in Lory's words: 35 scenes (lights, materials, textures, shadows, fog, instancing, clipping, picking, fly, trackball and drag controls, and in a headset dragging, a ball shooter, paint and teleport), 24 held to three 0.187.0's pictures, 22 the same in every pixel. |
| [scene](scene/) | A sphere, a box and a column over a ground, lit by the sky and the sun, seen by an orbiting camera. In a headset, it stands on the floor in front of you. |
| [materials](materials/) | Metal and non-metal balls of rising roughness, a box, the sun's shadows, and a ball drawn at the detail its distance asks for. |
| [babylon](babylon/) | Babylon.js's first Playground scene, stated in Lory's words and held to Babylon's picture. |
| [drift](drift/) | Lantern Drift, an original game: catch the motes of light and dodge the embers. Play with the keys, by dragging, or with a headset's stick or ray. |
