# 3D shapes and scenes

Lory draws in three dimensions. `lory.geometry` builds shapes vertex by
vertex: boxes, spheres, cylinders, tori and knots, capsules, lathes, the
regular solids, tubes along curves, flat outlines with holes, extrusions with
bevels, and surfaces found by marching cubes. `lory.space` holds those shapes
on the page and draws them lit, through a camera, behind the flat drawing of
panels and labels. It can also cast rays, test bounds and find what the
pointer is over. `lory.controls` moves the camera or the things in the
scene, and `lory.flip` turns a panel of controls in 3D to show its next side.

Distances are in metres. On a screen, the camera orbits a point: drag to turn
it and use the wheel to come nearer. A trackball turns the scene every way. A
fly camera moves with W, A, S, D, R and F, rolls with Q and E, and turns with
the arrow keys or by holding the pointer down. The pointer can take a thing
and drag it over the plane that faces the eye.

## Components

| Component | What it does |
|---|---|
| Shapes | Box, plane, circle, ring, sphere, cylinder, cone, torus, torus knot, capsule, lathe and the regular solids, each with its size and segment counts |
| Curves and tubes | Lines, quadratic and cubic curves, splines and helices, measured along, with a tube swept along any of them |
| Outlines and extrusions | Flat paths with holes, filled or extruded with a bevel or along a path; SVG path data read into them |
| Edges and wireframes | The edges where faces meet at more than an angle, or every triangle's edges, drawn as lines |
| Marching cubes | A surface found in a sampled field, such as a group of balls |
| Meshes and forms | Shapes held on the page and drawn as triangles, lines or points, with a colour for each vertex |
| Looks | Plain and physically based surfaces, plus unlit, matte, glossy and facing-coloured materials; dashes, points, blending, pictures and alpha cut-outs |
| Lamps | Ambient, sky-and-ground, directional, point and spot lights, with shadows from one lamp |
| Fog | Linear fog or a haze that thickens with depth |
| Cameras and views | Perspective and orthographic lenses, a camera that orbits a point, several views side by side |
| Copies | Many copies of one shape drawn at once, each placed and coloured on its own |
| Levels of detail | The mesh a thing's distance asks for, chosen from finer and coarser ones |
| Picking | Rays from the pointer or a controller, and what they hit: triangles, lines and points |
| Bounds and culling | Boxes and spheres that hold a shape, and whether a camera sees it |
| Helpers | Axes, a grid on the ground, a box's outline and an arrow |
| Fly, trackball and drag controls | A camera flown through the scene or turned every way, and things dragged by the pointer |
| Flip shape | A panel of sides that turns like a cube or a card to show the next |

## Using it

```crow
use sys
use lory
use lory.space
use lory.geometry
use lory.controls

fn draw[frame]
    c = lory.begin[frame]
    if not space.made['knot']
        c = space.mesh_form[c, 'knot', geometry.space_form[geometry.torus_knot[1.0, 0.4, 64, 8, 2.0, 3.0]]]
        c = space.mesh[c, 'ground', space.ground[8.0, 8.0]]
    lens_of = space.lens[50.0, space.proportion[c], 0.1, 100.0]
    cam = controls.trackball[c, 'view', lens_of, [0.0, 1.5, 0.0], [0.0, 3.0, 8.0]]
    c = space.through[c, cam, space.daylight[]]
    c = space.lamps[c, [space.ambient[lory.white[], 0.3], space.directional[lory.white[], 2.0, [2.0, 4.0, 3.0], [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]]]]
    c = space.draw[c, 'ground', lory.identity[], space.plain[lory.hex['#9ca3af']]]
    c = space.draw[c, 'knot', lory.translation[0.0, 1.5, 0.0], space.standard[lory.hex['#e8590c'], 0.2, 0.4]]
    lory.end[c]

sys.frames[draw]
```

## In a headset

A scene is drawn for each eye, and the camera becomes the reader's head. The
floor is at 0 and the reader stands at the origin. The controller's ray picks
things and its trigger takes them. The left stick walks and the right one
turns (`space.walk`), and `space.stand_at` teleports. In AR, the room shows
through wherever nothing is drawn.
