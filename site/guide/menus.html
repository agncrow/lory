Menus, dialogs and notifications

Three modules hold what opens over a page or under a trigger. lory.menus gives dropdown and context menus, a menubar, a navigation menu, a command palette and a floating action button with its speed dial. lory.overlays gives dialogs, popovers, hover cards, tooltips, toasts and snackbars, sheets, banners and dimmers. lory.disclosure gives the accordion, the collapsible and the tree view.

Everything answers the keyboard, the pointer and touch. Menus move with the arrows, Home and End, type ahead by first letters, and close on Escape or a press outside; a submenu opens after a short rest and stays open while the pointer heads for it. A context menu opens on a right press or a touch held 700 ms. Dialogs keep the focus and give it back when they close. Toasts pause while the pointer is on them, close on a swipe, and F8 takes the focus to the newest. Your program keeps what is open (a dialog's open, the folds open, the notices shown) and changes it on the events each frame tells; Lory keeps the small things inside, such as a menu's highlighted row, a tooltip's timers and a tree's open folders.

Components

ComponentWhat it does
Dropdown menuA button that opens a menu of items, checkboxes, radio items, labels, separators and submenus
Context menuA menu that opens at the pointer on a right press, or a touch held still
MenubarA bar of menu titles; the arrows move between menus once one is open
Navigation menuA site's bar whose items open panels of links and controls, or act as links
Command paletteA searchable list of commands in a dialog, ranked as you type, opened with Ctrl-K or Command-K
Floating action buttonA round button at a corner of the page
Speed dialA floating button that opens a column of actions above it
DialogA modal panel in the middle of the page, with a close button
Alert dialogA dialog that asks before an act; its focus starts on Cancel
PopoverA button that opens a panel of controls beside it
Hover cardA link whose card opens after the pointer rests on it
TooltipA short tip on a control, opened by resting on it or focusing it
ToastsNotices stacked in a corner, each with its own time, an act and a close
SnackbarsNotices at the foot of the page, one at a time
SheetA panel that slides in from a side of the page
BannerA strip across the page with an icon, words and acts
DimmerA dimming over a box or the whole page, with content in its middle
AccordionFolds under one another, one or many open at a time
CollapsibleA trigger that shows and hides what follows it
Tree viewFolders and leaves with the arrow keys, typing ahead and optional checkboxes

use sys
use memory
use lory
use lory.gui
use lory.menus
use lory.overlays
use lory.disclosure

struct App [renaming, folds, notices]

fn page[c, a]
    notes = [menus.item['new', 'New note', shortcut = 'Ctrl+N'], menus.item['rename', 'Rename…'], menus.separator[], menus.submenu['share', 'Share', [menus.item['mail', 'Mail']]]]
    bar = menus.menubar[c, 'bar', [menus.bar_menu['file', 'File', notes]]]
    faq = disclosure.accordion[c, 'faq', [disclosure.fold['what', 'What is this?', [gui.text['A notes editor.']]]], a.folds, collapsible = true]
    tree = disclosure.tree[c, 'files', [disclosure.branch['notes', 'notes', [disclosure.leaf['ideas', 'ideas.md']]]], checks = true]
    rename = overlays.dialog[c, 'rename', 'Rename note', [gui.field['title', 'Ideas']], a.renaming]
    gui.column[[bar, faq, tree, overlays.toasts[c, 'shelf', a.notices], rename], pad = 16.0]

fn draw[frame]
    a = recall['notes', App[false, ['what'], [overlays.notice['hello', 'Welcome']]]]
    c = lory.begin[frame]
    shown = gui.show[c, page[c, a]]
    a.folds = disclosure.folds_open[shown, 'faq', a.folds]
    for e in shown.events
        if e.act eq 'choose' and e.words eq 'rename'
            a.renaming = true
        if e.act eq 'dismiss' and e.id eq 'rename'
            a.renaming = false
    remember['notes', a]
    lory.end[shown.canvas]

sys.frames[draw]



Choosing an item tells a choose event naming the menu and the item; the
dialog's close button, Escape and a press on the dimming tell dismiss, and
the program closes it.

The page stands on a panel in the room and the controller's ray presses as
the pointer does: menus open and choose, dialogs close from their button,
and a tree opens its folders just as on a page.
